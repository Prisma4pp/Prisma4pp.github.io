What you can do

Log and rate

Mark games as completed, currently playing, or on your backlog. Rate them with stars and write reviews to remember what you thought of each one.

Lists and tier lists

Create your own game lists (favorites, backlog, by genre...) and organize your personal rankings with visual, easy-to-share tier lists.

Discover your next game

Get recommendations based on what you've already played and rated, explore recent releases, and check out full game pages.

Community and friends

Follow your friends, compare your lists and rankings, and take part in polls to see what the community thinks about your favorite games.

Personal stats

Check out your gaming activity: how many games you've played, your most frequent ratings, your drop-off rate, and your favorite genres and platforms.

Never miss a thing

Turn on notifications to hear about releases, friends' polls, and other updates relevant to you.

Prisma is made for anyone who loves video games and wants to keep an organized record of their hobby, discover new titles, and share it all with their community.
